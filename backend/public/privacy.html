YTIQ — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

YTIQ ("the app", "we") is a YouTube growth tool made by Udaan Beyond. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

What we access

  • Your Google account email, only to tell your connected channels apart.
  • Your YouTube channel data through Google sign-in: channel name, subscribers, views, your videos and their statistics (titles, descriptions, tags, views, likes, comments).
  • Actions you ask for: uploading a video, setting a thumbnail, and editing or deleting a video's details. The app does these only when you tap the matching button.
  • Videos you choose in the app: their audio, a few frames, and text made from them, so the app can write a title, description and tags.

How we use it

Only to give you the app's features: show your channel's numbers, suggest titles and ideas, upload and edit your videos, and (if you connect it) let an AI assistant such as Claude read your channel statistics. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use your data to train AI models.

AI services

To write text, the app sends the video's audio or transcript, frames, and your channel's titles and statistics to AI providers: Google Gemini and Cloudflare Workers AI. If you connect the YTIQ connector to Claude or ChatGPT, that assistant receives the channel statistics you ask it about.

Where data is stored

  • On your phone: your login tokens (encrypted), the app's memory of your searches and edits, and cached YouTube answers. Logging out of a channel in the app removes its login from the phone.
  • On our server (ytiq.pages.dev): only if you connect the assistant connector. Your Google refresh token is stored encrypted and used only to read your channel statistics when you use the connector.

Google API Services User Data Policy

YTIQ's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

You can revoke YTIQ's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Logging out in the app removes the login from your phone. To delete anything stored on our server, remove the connector in your assistant and contact us.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Contact: udaanbeyond.com.
